# 🌟 Highlight Block

**Highlight Block** is an ultra-performant, fair, and lightweight client-side Fabric mod for Minecraft (26.2+). It instantly transforms any targeted block into a **high-visibility glowing yellow exclamation mark (!)** with a single keypress, making it effortless to spot specific blocks while mining, building, or exploring!

---

### ✨ Features

* **🎯 Look & Press Hotkey (`H`):**
  Simply look at any block you want to spot (e.g. Andesite, Deepslate Diamond Ore, Ancient Debris) and press **`H`**. The texture dynamically swaps to a glowing yellow exclamation mark. Pressing **`H`** again immediately restores its original appearance.
* **⚡ 0% FPS Drops & Extreme Render Distance:**
  Unlike shader-based outlines or heavy entity renderers that cause massive frame drops beyond 32 blocks, Highlight Block manages a seamless dynamic texture system. Enjoy full framerates even at 64+ chunks render distance with Sodium, Iris, and Distant Horizons!
* **💡 High Contrast & Cave Visibility:**
  Engineered with high-contrast neon/gold yellow on a deep dark border, the exclamation mark pops out even in pitch-black caves and ravines.
* **🚫 100% Fair Play (No X-Ray / Wallhacks):**
  Only visible block faces are highlighted. Hidden blocks behind solid stone are never revealed, making it fully legitimate for fair multiplayer gameplay.
* **⚙️ Mod Menu Integration & Commands:**
  Includes a built-in config screen compatible with **Mod Menu** or quick chat commands:
  * `/hb gui` or `/hb config` – Opens the in-game settings screen
  * `/hb list` – Lists all currently highlighted blocks
  * `/hb clear` – Clears all active block selections
* **🔌 True Out-of-the-Box Experience:**
  No manual folder creation, zip extraction, or resourcepack menu tinkering required. Drop the `.jar` into your `mods` folder, and you are ready to go.

---

### 🎮 Default Controls
* **`H`**: Toggle highlight texture on the targeted block

*(Keybinding can be easily customized in Minecraft's standard Options -> Controls -> Key Binds under "Highlight Block").*
